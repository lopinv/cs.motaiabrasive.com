우리의 사업

허베이 모타이 연마재 유한공사는 목공·금속·자동차용 연마재 소모품을 공급합니다. 문서화된 라인업: 무홀 샌딩 디스크, 메시 샌딩 디스크, 빨간 연마 벨트. 수입업자·유통업자·도매상·산업 구매자의 문의를 받습니다 — 제품, 백킹, 크기, 등급, 수량, 납품 요구사항.

후베이에서 귀사의 시장까지

목적 지역별로 조직된 글로벌 공급.

Europe

Note size, grit, backing and product files; provide destination port and label language.

North America

State inch or metric sizing, attachment type and pack quantities; destination ZIP or port and transport mode.

South America

Size, mixed-grit quantities and carton label language; destination port and importer documents.

Middle East

Material, grit and moisture-safe packaging; destination port and quotation terms.

Africa

Machine compatibility, required sizes and pack quantities; destination port, carton marks and transport preference.

Oceania

Size, backing, packaging and destination ZIP or port; import and packing requirements.

Asia

Machine specification, grit range and order mix; delivery city or port and local-language labels.

시작하기 전에

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ comes back with the offer, alongside availability and terms.

Do you support OEM and private label?

Yes. Logo, labels, custom sizes and packing are checked for feasibility, then confirmed with your offer.

Which sizes and grits do you supply?

Belts in documented sizes from 533 × 75 to 2200 × 1330 mm (grits 40–800); discs 3–6 inch (grits 60–5000). Custom requirements are reviewed with our team.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. Request a sample, and our sales team will confirm availability, charges and shipping arrangements.

What are the lead times?

Lead time depends on specification and quantity, and is confirmed in your quotation.

How are products packed?

Pack quantities, labels, carton marks and private-label artwork are confirmed with the quotation.

Which payment terms do you offer?

Payment terms are part of the confirmed quotation and commercial discussion.
