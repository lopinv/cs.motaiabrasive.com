### FAQ

**What should I look for in a sanding pad manufacturer before I buy?** Four things, in order: the grit standard he names (FEPA or ISO 848), the bond he will sample against the lot you will buy, the belt line that tells you his transfer discipline, and the rated speed on the spec sheet. If all four come back clean, run a small sample on your own machine and count the cost per surface before the full order.

**Hook and loop or PSA: which mount should I buy for an orbital sander?** The mandrel decides. A hook and loop mandrel takes loop discs, and the whole grit ladder can live on one mount with a seconds-long swap; a PSA setup takes pressure-sensitive discs that stay flat under pressure. Many shops keep both: the loop mount for the grit ladder, PSA for the fine finishing pass where a flat, stuck pad wants to sit still.

**Mesh pad or Holeless Pad on a random orbital?** Mesh for the dust-heavy work: MDF, particleboard, long tops. The holeless style suits the final, fine pass where the vacuum is strong, the area is small and the evenness of a continuous face is what you want, such as a P320 or P400 before a finish.

**When does a Perforated Pad beat a mesh one?** When the cut is fine and the dust is light: painted surfaces, metal, the last step of a refinish. A drilled face keeps more of the grain working and leaves a more uniform fine scratch, while mesh keeps the face open when the dust load is heavy.

**What does grit 2400 actually do on a panel?** It closes. On a surface that the P1500 step has already prepared, the final fine pass removes the last micro scratches and the nibs before the polish; it will not remove stock. Light hand, moving pad, clean face, and keep it off the coarse work, where it will glaze and quit.

**Which grit standard should I name in the enquiry?** FEPA or ISO 848, whichever scale your shop already runs, and keep the whole shop on that one scale. The difference shows up in a 150 that is not a 150; the reference is at [the FEPA grit standard (ISO 848)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FEPA), and the full public reference library is at [ISO standards](https://www.iso.org/).

**What lead time should I plan for a private label pad order?** It is two numbers: the run, which is fast on stock grits and backings and longer on a custom coat, and your own sample approval, which most shops want on a branded run. Confirm the MOQ and the lead time with sales on the [request a quotation](/quote) page, and keep the terms for the label language in the [abrasives glossary](/glossary) so the shop and the maker are reading the same words.


### Before you buy

A short compliance and purchasing checklist. It is a reminder, not a substitute for your own site rules, and in compliant use, with the right protection, the risk of these tools is reduced, not removed.

- 🧤 **Confirm the tool's max speed against the product's rated speed** before every use; where the number is not printed, confirm it with sales and keep the tool below it.
- **Check the mandrel and the mount fit**, PSA face to paper, loop to hook, and keep the tool's guard on at all times.
- **Wear eye and face protection**, and the respiratory protection that matches the dust load; the baseline is [OSHA 29 CFR 1910.132 — PPE general requirements](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.132).
- **Run dust extraction at the work**, with a filter matched to the material, and plan the waste path, not just the dust path.
- **Name the grit standard in the enquiry**, FEPA or ISO 848, plus the grit, the size, the mount and the backing, so two suppliers cannot ship you two different pads.
- **Ask for the inspection report and the batch record** for the lot you are buying, and for a sample against the spec on any new maker.
- **Store and transport pads face-up or wrapped**, away from heat, so the PSA faces do not bond in the warehouse or the container.
- **Follow the maker's install notes** for the mount and the speed, and re-check the guard and the mandrel each shift; setup practice is at [CCOHS "Abrasive Wheels" occupational health answers](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/abrasive_wheels).
- **Start the order on the quotation page**: confirm MOQ, lead time and packing for any [private label run](/oem), and begin on [request a quotation](/quote).
