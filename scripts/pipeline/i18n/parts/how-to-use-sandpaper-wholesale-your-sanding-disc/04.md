### FAQ

**What grit do I start with on a panel that has been filled?** P150 to P180 to take the filler scratch down, then P320 to P400 to bring the whole surface into one band before the next coat. Start coarser than the finish you want and step down, and do not jump two steps.

**What is the difference between the disc formats?** PSA suits a tool where you change grit between coats; hook-and-loop suits a shop that changes grit every pass and wants the disc face to stay clean between changes; mesh suits curves; non-hole suits a fixed head on a long single-grit run. Match the format to the tool, then match the grit to the material.

**Can I run a 6 inch disc on the same random orbital as a 4 inch disc?** Usually yes, but confirm the tool's rated speed against the disc's printed maximum and confirm the tool head takes the size, because the larger diameter reaches a higher line speed on the same tool. When in doubt, check the tool rating and the disc label before you fit it.

**Why does my disc stop cutting or look glazed?** Usually loading or glazing, or a grit too fine for the material, or a grain that packs on what you are cutting. Step the grit, move to a tougher grain, and keep the tool moving instead of pressing in. A water-graded disc on a water-borne coat is the specific fix for the glazed face.

**What does P1200 do that P400 does not?** P1200 de-raises and refines a surface that is already close to its finish line; P400 does the real blending and prep. You run P400 first and P1200 on top, not the other way around. That is the blocking band on a panel before the top coat.

**How do I buy Sandpaper Wholesale or in bulk?** Send grain, grit, format, size, the grit standard (FEPA or ISO 848) and the quantity to a supplier; the [Sandpaper Wholesale](/collections/sanding-discs) range and the [request a quotation](/quote) page are where a bulk or private label order starts. Confirm the minimum order and the lead time with sales before the order is final.

**What standard should I name in an enquiry?** Name FEPA or ISO 848 grit plus the format and the size; that keeps your order from being mixed with a grit scale you are not buying to. The reference is at [the FEPA grit standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FEPA), and it is one of the fields the quotation form asks for.

**Waterproof or water-resistant — which do I spec?** Waterproof is the spec when the job runs the disc under water or over a water-borne coat; water-resistant is the spec when the disc meets water as part of the sequence but not as its working medium. Both come off the same grits, so the choice is the job, not the grain.


### Before you buy

A short compliance and purchasing checklist. This is a reminder, not a substitute for your own site rules, and in compliant use with the right protection the risk of these tools is reduced but not zero.

- 🔢 Confirm the tool's no-load speed is at or below the disc's rated maximum working speed before the first pass, and confirm the rated speed against the model you are ordering, not against a similar one on the shelf.
- ⚙️ Check the tool head and disc format match: hook-and-loop on a loop patch, PSA on a clean head, mesh or non-hole on the head it is cut for. A disc that does not sit fully on the head is the failure mode that starts at the bench.
- 🛡️ Fit the tool guard and keep it on; it is the first barrier against a disc that separates or rides loose.
- 👓 Wear eye and face protection on every dry pass, and add respiratory protection matched to the material dust, plus dust extraction at the tool for every long run. The U.S. PPE baseline is [OSHA 29 CFR 1910.132](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.132), and the setup side is at [CCOHS: Abrasive Wheels](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/abrasive_wheels).
- 📐 Name the grit standard, FEPA or ISO 848, in the enquiry, and keep the whole programme on that one scale so restocks match.
- 📄 Ask for the inspection report and the certificate that backs the grain and grit you are ordering; the fields to confirm are grain, grit, format, size and the rated speed, and the report is where those numbers live.
- 📦 Plan storage and transport on the spec: the disc is a coated product with a grain and a bond, so the storage is dry, flat and away from heat, not the corner of a wet shop.
- 🔩 Follow the install notes on the format you buy, and check the patch or the head wear before a long run, because a worn patch is the quiet way a disc rides loose at speed.
- 💧 If the sequence uses a water step, confirm the water-graded stock for that step and keep it off the dry-grit shelf, so the water-graded disc and the dry disc do not get mixed on repack.
