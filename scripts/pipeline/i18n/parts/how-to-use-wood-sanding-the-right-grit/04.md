### Before you buy 🧾

The checklist to run before a Wood Sanding order goes out, or before the first panel of the day. It is a reminder, not a substitute for your own site rules, and under compliant speed, correct fit and PPE, the risk of these machines is reduced, not zero.

- Confirm the machine platen speed is at or below the belt rated speed before every use; we confirm the rated speed against the model, so ask for the figure on the belt you order.
- Check the belt width and length against the platen, and the pad diameter against the sander's arbor, before the abrasive touches the wood.
- Keep the machine guard on and the drive locked out for any change; the guard is the first defence against a belt or pad that lets go.
- Wear eye and face protection and the right respiratory protection for the dust load; the U.S. PPE baseline is at [OSHA 29 CFR 1910.132](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.132).
- Run dust extraction at the platen, and store the waste dust away from the bench rather than in the corner of the shop.
- Name the grit standard in the enquiry, FEPA or ISO 848, and state the grit, width, length, grain and model so two suppliers cannot ship you two different cuts.
- Ask for the inspection report or certificate batch that sits with the order, and check it against the spec sheet before you accept the roll.
- Follow the install notes on the label, and check the panel for scratch depth and heat on thin or veneered stock before you commit the batch.
- Confirm lead time, minimum order and packing for any [abrasive belt range](/collections/abrasive-belts) or [private label order](/oem) run, and start with the [quotation form](/quote).

Tell us the wood, the finish you are aiming for and the tool you run, and the team will pre-select the grit and the backing to start from; send those three and we will work the sequence with you on the [request a quotation](/quote) page.
