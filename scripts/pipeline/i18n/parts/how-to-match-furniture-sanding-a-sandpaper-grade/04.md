### Before you buy

A short compliance and purchasing checklist. It is a reminder, not a substitute for the site's own rules, and under compliant use with the right protection the risk of these tools is reduced, not zero.

- 🛡️ Confirm the tool's maximum speed sits at or below the printed rated speed of the disc or belt being bought, and keep the tool below that number.
- ⚙️ Confirm the fit: the disc or belt has to match the tool, mandrel or platen it will run on, and where the sizes differ, a rated adapter goes on, not a modified one.
- 🎯 Confirm the tool's guard is fitted and sized for the abrasive; it is the first defence against a failed disc or belt.
- 👓 Wear eye and face protection, and run dust extraction with respiratory protection matched to the dust load.
- 📏 Confirm the grit standard, FEPA or ISO 848, plus grain, size and backing, in every enquiry so two suppliers cannot ship two different grits.
- 📄 Ask for the inspection report or batch certificate on bulk or OEM orders, and keep the one that ships with the delivery on file.
- 📦 Store the stock flat, dry and off the floor, check that the transit kept it that way, and report any crushed cartons before they reach the bench.
- ✅ Follow the maker's installation notes for each tool, and check the panel for heat and scratch depth after the first pass on soft or figured wood.
- 💸 Confirm the MOQ, lead time, packing and any label work on a bulk or private label order, and start with [request a quotation](/quote).
