### FAQ 💬

**What grit do I start with for general grinding on steel?** In many cases P80 to P120 covers everyday grinding and deburring, then step down to P240 or P400 to blend and finish. Start coarse, work fine, and do not skip two steps at once.

**What does P60 do that a P120 does not?** P60 sits at the coarse end of the working scale: it strips a weld cap, opens the face of a casting and moves stock a fine grit would just pack on. It leaves deep scratches, so it is the disc you start with, not the one you end with.

**Which backing suits a fast grinder?** A fiberglass backing takes the heat and the flex of a faster tool, a steel backing is the pick where the job wants a firm, rigid cut under pressure, and a plastic backing is the answer where the pass is light and the part should stay cool. Match the backing to the speed and the job, then set the grit to the cut.

**Can I mount a flap disc on an abrasive belt machine?** No. The two are different tools: a belt is a closed loop that wants a platen or a drum, and a flap disc wants to spin on a grinder's arbor. Clamping a disc to a platen wears it unevenly and overheats it. Run the disc on the grinder and the belt on the belt sander, and keep each one on the job it is shaped for.

**Aluminium oxide or zirconia alumina on a stainless weld?** Aluminium oxide is the safe default on steel and stainless; the open grain is the pick where the cap packs the face and a cut that stays open matters more than the longest life. Match the grain to the material, then set the grit to the cut.

**What standard should I name in an order?** FEPA or ISO 848, plus the grit, the diameter and the arbor. Naming the standard stops two suppliers from quietly quoting two different scales — the reference is at [the FEPA grit standard (ISO 848)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FEPA).

**How do I buy in bulk, or on my own label?** Send grit, backing, diameter, arbor and the standard, and start on the [request a quotation](/quote) page. A branded run goes through [the OEM & private-label service page](/oem), with the label, the packing and the spec sheet defined up front and a sample checked against the spec before the full order.

**How long does a flap disc last?** That is the honest one, because it moves with the material, the pressure, the speed and the backing. The number that holds up across jobs is cost per finished part: when life on one disc looks short, check the pressure first, then the backing, and buy on the per part number instead of the sticker price. Where the job is steady volume, confirm the lead time and the minimum order quantity with sales so the shelf does not run dry.


### Before you buy ✅

A short compliance and purchasing checklist. This is a reminder, not a substitute for your own site rules, and in compliant use with the right protection the risk of these tools is reduced but not zero.

- 🛠️ Confirm your tool's maximum speed sits below the disc's printed maximum working speed before every use; where the two do not line up, drop the tool speed or the disc diameter.
- 🧩 Check the arbor hole and the flange are the correct size, use a rated adapter where needed, and keep the guard fitted and the right size for the disc on every pass.
- 🧤 Wear eye and face protection at the tool, and add respiratory protection matched to the dust load of the material you are grinding.
- 🌫️ Run dust extraction at the work, not at the far end of the bay, and give the waste somewhere to go that is not a corner of the floor.
- 📏 Name the grit standard in every order — FEPA or ISO 848, with the grit, diameter, arbor and backing on the same line.
- 📋 Ask for the certification and the inspection report behind the batch, and confirm the maximum working speed against the model before it ships.
- 📦 Store the discs flat, dry and away from heat, and keep the flaps closed to the backing through transport.
- 🧯 Follow the install notes on the disc and the tool: guard size, flange and rotation direction all matter on the first fit.
- Confirm lead time, minimum order quantity and packing for any bulk or branded run, and start on the [request a quotation](/quote) page.
