FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions buyers ask before ordering this product.

Which specifications should I provide?

Share your required dimensions, grit, workpiece, machine and intended finish. Use the documented options where available; other requirements will be reviewed with our team.

How do I request OEM packaging?

Include your branding and packaging requirements in the enquiry. Details will be reviewed and confirmed with your quotation.

Does requesting a quote place an order?

No. The form starts a wholesale enquiry. Product specifications, availability and commercial terms are confirmed before an order is agreed.

Can I test a sample before committing to a full order?

Yes. Samples of standard references are available for evaluation on request; sample availability and freight terms are confirmed with your quotation.

How far ahead should I place a production order?

Lead time starts from the date the quotation is confirmed in writing and depends on the size, grit and packaging ordered. Stock references ship fastest.
